Supabase Connected Architecture

Database & Storage Sync

Account, trip, route stop, wishlist, and checklist updates route through window.YatraAccountStore + window.YatraSupabase:

  • auth → supabase.auth + JWT session
  • profile → public.profiles + profile-images bucket
  • trips → public.trips, trip_stops, itineraries & budgets
  • checklist → public.packing_checklists
  • wishlist & reviews → public.wishlists & public.reviews
